# RN2 Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We pledge to maintain a respectful, inclusive, and safe environment for all contributors.

## Our Standards
- Use welcoming and inclusive language
- Respect differing viewpoints
- Accept constructive criticism
- Focus on what benefits the community

## Unacceptable Behavior
- Harassment
- Personal attacks
- Sharing private data
- Malicious pattern injection

## Enforcement
Violations may result in warnings, temporary bans, or permanent removal from the project.
